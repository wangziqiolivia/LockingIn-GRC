# LockedIn-GRC
AI enhanced website design, based on designs in OpenNotes GRC.

## Vision
We're revolutionizing what socializing can be like on campus. Boundless. Collaborative. 

With the help of the internet and socializing platforms like discord, we're now able to connect everyone on campus and form a direct communicating community.

Whether you need help academically, want to find students to study together, join competitions together, or start a youtube channel together, you can find all you need here!

### Academic sections
We know there are many students who would like to become tutors but don't have the opportunity. They are generous with their knowledge and hope to lift up their peers with their abilities. Plus, tutoring is the best way to reinforce what you have learned, and even discover new questions that you haven't thought about. Tutors also practice the ability to explain things clearly and vividly, as well as patience and compassion, which are essential to communication.

There are also many students who struggle in their classes, however. A lot of the times, they fall behind not because they're not hard-working enough; it's because of many factors like financial hardships, work responsibilities, coming from underprivileged backgrounds, etc. They will thrive if we provide them with mentoring and resources that they are previously not exposed to.

Other times, students fall behind because they are afraid to ask professors questions. It might be that the teaching style or personality of the professor is a bit harsh, or mental fears from childhood. This is very prevalent but often unseen. 

Our mission is to remove any barriers that keep students from realizng their dreams and academic goals. We do so by providing this online platform for students to share and receive knowledge.

# Words from heart
We also want to spread the notion that knowledge is best to be shared. This is how mankind developed into such an advanced civilization. Over thousands of years of history, people fail, learn, and then teach their descendents. 

We still face so many issues in our societies nowadays. It is our mission to tackle them, and build a society of freedom, abundance and happiness. Humans should unite as brothers and sisters, not compete, discriminate, or fight like enemies.

Let's start from uniting in knowledge. Learn. Share. Innovate.
