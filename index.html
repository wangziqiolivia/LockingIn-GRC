Why Locking In?

Sometimes getting academic support isn't about needing more resources. It's about making it easier to ask for help.

01

“I'm scared to ask my professor questions.”

Totally okay! Talking to professors can be stressful for us students sometimes. Reaching out to peer tutors can feel much easier!

02

“I want to ask my classmates, but I'm worried I might bother them.”

Our tutors are completely voluntary and willing to dedicate their time to helping their peers.

03

“I'm too shy to ask questions in person.”

We got you! You can choose to ask questions through chat online, and tutors can help in the way you both prefer. Any learning style is great if it works for you!

04

“I want to form a study group, but I don't know who has the same need.”

Join us on Discord! Post in our study group channel and students from the whole college who are interested can reply to your message.

05

“I want to tutor, but the tutoring center has no openings.”

That's awesome! You can become a peer tutor here. Students in our college will be able to find you, and your help can make a real difference.

Questions? We got you.

A few things students often wonder about.

I'm nervous about asking questions in person.

We got you! You can choose any format that feels comfortable, including asking questions online.

I'm not confident that I can tutor.

No worries! You don't need to know everything to tutor, and you won't be giving serious lectures onstage. You can help in many ways including explaining homework problems, sharing notes you took, or even share study advice.

What if I only have one quick homework question?

That's exactly what the quick-question forum is for. You don't need to commit to a long-term tutoring arrangement.

How do I form a study group?

Join our Discord, make a post, and find classmates/students who want to work together.
