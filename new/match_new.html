How matching works

Four simple steps to find someone who fits what you're looking for.

01

Register

Register your information through our form.

Want to be both a tutor and tutee? Please register twice!

02

Browse

Look through our match sheet and find someone you'd like to connect with.

03

Reach out

Send an email from your student email with the name of the person you'd like to contact.

04

Connect

Our staff will contact them, usually within 24 hours.

If both sides agree, we'll help exchange emails.

📌

A few things to know

You're always in control of your match.

01

You're welcome to reach out to multiple people if you don't match the first time.

02

If someone doesn't want to connect, no information will be shared with you.

03

If you no longer want to be matched, you can ask our staff to delete or pause your information.
