🧑‍🏫

For students who want to help

We know there are many students who would like to become tutors but don't have the opportunity.

They are generous with their knowledge and hope to lift up their peers with their abilities.

Tutoring is also one of the best ways to reinforce what you have learned, and even discover new questions that you haven't thought about.

Tutors practice explaining things clearly and vividly, as well as patience and compassion — essential skills for communication.

🌱

For students who need support

There are also many students who struggle in their classes.

A lot of the time, they fall behind not because they're not hard-working enough.

Financial hardships, work responsibilities, coming from underprivileged backgrounds, and many other factors can create barriers.

Students can thrive when we provide them with mentoring and resources that they were previously not exposed to.

💬

For students who are afraid to ask

Other times, students fall behind because they are afraid to ask professors questions.

It might be that a teaching style or personality feels intimidating, or because of fears carried from childhood.

This is very prevalent, but often unseen.

✦
🎯 Our Mission

Remove the barriers.

Our mission is to remove any barriers that keep students from realizing their dreams and academic goals.

We do so by providing this online platform for students to share and receive knowledge.
